Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

Tracki GPS Tracker Guide is an offline reference app. It does not require an account, does not collect the content you enter, and does not know who you are — and it does not track your location. This page explains the little data that does move — mostly generated by the advertising that keeps the app free.

Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the "Tracki GPS Tracker Guide" Android app ("the app"), developed as an independent, unofficial companion guide for the Tracki Mini GPS Tracker. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Tracki. This app is a reference guide only — it does not connect to, control, or receive location data from your Tracki device. Location tracking of the device itself happens exclusively in Tracki's own official app and account system.

Information we do not collect

The app does not ask you to sign in, and does not collect or store:

  • Your name, email address, or phone number
  • Your location, or the location of any tracker device
  • Photos, videos, or files from your device
  • Any content you type into the app (there is nothing to type — it's a reference guide)

Information collected automatically

The app shows ads through Google AdMob to stay free. To serve and measure those ads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects limited technical data from your device, including:

DataPurpose
Advertising IDPersonalizing and measuring ads, and limiting how often you see the same one
Device and app informationDevice model, OS version, app version, language, and general (non-precise) location inferred from IP address
Ad interaction dataWhich ads were shown, viewed, or clicked

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us — we never see or store it ourselves. You can review how Google handles this data in the Google Ads Privacy & Terms.

Your ad choices

You can limit ad personalization at any time:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → "Opt out of Ads Personalization"
  • Reset your advertising ID from the same menu at any time

Permissions the app requests

The app requests internet access only, which is needed to load ads. It does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage, or any other device permission. Activating and locating your actual Tracki device happens in Tracki's own official app, governed by Tracki's own privacy policy — not this one.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us using the details below and we will address it.

Data sharing

We do not sell your data. The only third party involved is Google (AdMob), acting as an advertising service provider, as described above.

Data retention

Since we don't operate our own servers or accounts, we don't retain any personal data ourselves. Data retention for advertising identifiers is governed by Google's own policies, linked above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will change accordingly. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
